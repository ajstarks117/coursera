# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone. All contributors and participants are expected to interact professionally and respectfully, regardless of background, experience, identity, or personal characteristics.

We pledge to contribute to an open, inclusive, and positive community where everyone can participate and share ideas freely.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming different opinions and perspectives.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting feedback professionally.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or derogatory comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual language, imagery, or attention.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers and community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

They may take appropriate action in response to behavior that is inappropriate, offensive, threatening, or harmful to the community.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other official project communication channels.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated fairly and, where possible, promptly.

Project maintainers will respect the privacy of individuals who report incidents and will handle reports responsibly.

Depending on the severity of a violation, appropriate corrective action may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction from project activities, or permanent removal from the community.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the nature, severity, and circumstances of each violation when determining appropriate action.

Possible enforcement actions include:

### 1. Correction

For minor violations, the contributor may receive a private or public reminder about the expected standards of behavior.

### 2. Warning

For repeated or more serious violations, the contributor may receive a formal warning and be informed that further violations may result in additional restrictions.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

For serious violations, access to project discussions, contributions, or other community activities may be temporarily restricted.

### 4. Permanent Restriction

For severe or repeated violations, the contributor may be permanently restricted from participating in the project or community.
